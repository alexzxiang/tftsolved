# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Software-engineering and ML recruiters and hiring managers who reach the page from Alex's resume. They skim the top in under a minute, looking for evidence of engineering and modelling rigour; the engineers among them read on into the methods and open the public code. TFT players are a secondary audience.

## Product Purpose
A public case study for TFTSolved, a line finder for Teamfight Tactics (Set 19) that ranks the lines a player's board can reach, with every weight traced to measured data. The page explains what the tool does, how it decides (the techniques and how each was validated), what the data overruled, and how it was built responsibly. Success: a reader leaves knowing what was built, which methods it uses, and that the claims are backed by numbers and public code. The page serves no third-party data.

## Brand Personality
Rigorous, understated, precise. It reads like a sharp research write-up rather than a pitch: confident numbers stated plainly, the honest admissions of what went wrong kept in, and the game's colour carried by the app's own screenshots rather than by decoration. Emotional goal: quiet credibility.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS / AI-startup landing pages: gradient blobs, icon-card grids, hero metrics, fake testimonials, "revolutionary" copy.
- Busy esports and tier-list sites: ads, banners, dense tables, neon everywhere.
- Glassmorphism, gradient text, section eyebrows on every heading.

## Design Principles
- Evidence over adjectives: every claim carries a number, a method, or a screenshot.
- Show the product: the app's own UI is the hero imagery.
- Admit the misses: what the data overruled is part of the story, not hidden.
- Respect the reader's time: the first screen answers what it is and why it's credible.
- Practise what it preaches: precise, fast, accessible, no filler.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast at least 4.5:1 (3:1 for large text), visible focus, real alt text on every screenshot, semantic headings and landmarks, and a reduced-motion alternative for any animation. Fully usable on mobile.
